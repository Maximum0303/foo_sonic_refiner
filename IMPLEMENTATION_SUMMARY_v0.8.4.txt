Sonic Refiner v0.8.4

Base: validated v0.8.4-dev.1 source, itself based directly on official v0.8.3.

Purpose:
- Fix audible small/abrupt whole-signal level drops traced to Auto Headroom Protection, especially on quieter songs / ballads with Adaptive Standard (ATB).

Auto Headroom changes:
- Keep reference ceiling at 0.98 (approx. -0.2 dBFS).
- Replace block-wide instantaneous peak attenuation with a per-frame two-stage envelope detector.
- Stage 1: immediate peak capture with ~0.03 s peak-envelope release.
- Stage 2: sustained-peak smoothing with ~0.08 s attack / ~0.20 s release.
- Protection gain uses ~0.15 s attack / ~0.45 s release.
- Brief transients normally do not cross the sustained detector threshold and therefore do not pull down the whole signal.
- Sustained high peaks are still attenuated, but the gain movement is gradual.
- This is not a brick-wall / True Peak limiter; downstream R128 remains responsible for final True Peak control.
