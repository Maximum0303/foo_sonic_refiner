Sonic Refiner v1.2.1 Implementation Summary
Date: 2026-10-16

Base:
- foo_sonic_refiner_v1.2.0_source_FINAL.zip
- SHA-256: 8f81895cba81cdcec320f15210fab369e4023507de93aacab522b2ecbbbe6d16

Purpose:
- Fix Processing Monitor window-position persistence when foobar2000 itself is closed.

Cause / timing risk:
- The v1.2.0 Monitor writes its position on WM_DESTROY and initquit::on_quit().
- foobar2000 may serialize cfg_* state before the quit-time assignment is persisted.
- Therefore the final position can be older than the visible Monitor position at shutdown.

Implementation:
- Added a WM_EXITSIZEMOVE handler to sonic_refiner_monitor_dialog.
- The handler calls save_processing_monitor_position(m_hWnd) immediately after the user finishes a move/resize loop.
- Existing WM_DESTROY and initquit::on_quit() position saves remain unchanged as fallback paths.
- No DSP or preset logic was changed.

Unchanged:
- DSP processing and coefficients.
- ATB logic/runtime state.
- Auto Headroom algorithm/constants.
- Width/Ambience/Reverb/tail.
- Master Strength/Output Gain/Level-Matched Bypass/A-B.
- All 18 built-in preset values.
- SRP5 / preset_version 10 / .srpbackup / legacy compatibility.
- Monitor telemetry, approximately 100 ms refresh, pause-display behavior, geometry, language behavior, Always on Top and Minimize with foobar2000 behavior.
